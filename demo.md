# Hotel Info Agent Demo

## Agent Playground

The Hotel Info Agent was tested using the Azure AI Foundry Agent Playground with common hotel guest questions.

## Hotel Amenities and Facilities

A guest asked about the hotel's amenities and facilities. The agent provided information about Wi-Fi, the fitness center, swimming pool, restaurant, room service, business center, laundry services, concierge desk, parking, and meeting rooms.

![Hotel Info Agent responding to a guest question about hotel amenities and facilities](../amenities.png)

## Restaurant Opening Hours

A guest asked about the hotel's restaurant opening hours. The agent explained the breakfast and dinner hours and also provided the room service hours.

![Hotel Info Agent providing restaurant and room service opening hours](../restaurant-hours.png)

## Pet Policy

A guest asked about the hotel's pet policy. The agent explained that pets are allowed in designated rooms, along with the pet limit, cleaning fee, and restrictions in areas such as the restaurant, fitness center, and swimming pool.

![Hotel Info Agent explaining the hotel's pet policy](../pet-policy.png)

## User Stories

### User Story 1

Sara is a hotel guest who wants to know what facilities are available before her stay. She asks the Hotel Info Agent about the hotel's amenities and receives information about Wi-Fi, the fitness center, swimming pool, restaurant, room service, and parking.

### User Story 2

David is planning a hotel stay with his pet. He uses the Hotel Info Agent to check the pet policy and learns about designated pet-friendly rooms, the number of pets allowed, the cleaning fee, and areas where pets are not permitted.
